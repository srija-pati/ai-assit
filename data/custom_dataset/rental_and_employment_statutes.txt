RENTAL AGREEMENT & EMPLOYMENT LAW GUIDELINES

1. RENTAL AND LEASE AGREEMENTS IN INDIA
- Rent Agreement Validity: Rent agreements for a period exceeding 11 months must be registered under the Registration Act 1908. Agreements up to 11 months do not require registration but are notarized.
- Security Deposit: Landlords typically collect 2 to 10 months of rent as refundable security deposit.
- Notice Period for Eviction: Landlords must provide at least 15 to 30 days written notice prior to initiating eviction proceedings.
- Maintenance Charges: Unless stated otherwise in the agreement, structural repairs are the responsibility of the landlord, while routine maintenance is borne by the tenant.

2. INDUSTRIAL EMPLOYMENT & INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS ACT
- Termination of Employment: Employees on permanent contracts are entitled to statutory notice (typically 30 days or salary in lieu of notice) unless terminated for gross misconduct following a proper enquiry.
- Non-Compete Clauses (Section 27 Indian Contract Act): Post-employment non-compete clauses restraining an employee from exercising a lawful profession, trade, or business are generally void under Indian Law (Percept D'Mark v. Zaheer Khan).
- Maternity Benefit Act: Women employees in organizations with 10 or more employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children.
